Monthly Business
Finance Tracker

A complete monthly financial dashboard for freelancers and solopreneurs. Track every euro in and out — and always know your real profit margin.

Setup Instructions

  1. Open a new Google Sheet. Rename it "Business Finance [Year]".
  2. Create 12 tabs — one per month (Jan, Feb, Mar...).
  3. Copy each table below into the corresponding section of your sheet.
  4. Enter your data in the yellow (input) cells. The green cells contain formulas — don't edit them.
  5. At month end: copy the entire sheet tab and rename it for the next month. Your formulas carry over.

Google Sheets Setup Guide

=SUM(range)
Add up any column of numbers. Example: =SUM(E2:E10)
=SUMIF(range,"Yes",sum_range)
Sum only rows where a condition is met. Used for "Deductible = Yes" and "Status = Paid".
=B2/B1
Profit margin: Gross Profit ÷ Total Revenue. Format cell as percentage.
Conditional Formatting
Select the "Status" column → Format → Conditional formatting → "Pending" = orange background, "Paid" = green. Instant visual dashboard.
Year Summary Tab
Create a 13th tab "Year Summary". Pull each month's totals with: =Jan!B_total_row (referencing the total row from each month tab). See your annual performance at a glance.

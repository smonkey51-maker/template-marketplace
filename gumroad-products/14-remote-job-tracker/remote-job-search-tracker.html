Job Search Tips Built Into This Tracker

→

Follow up after 7 days, not 3. Hiring managers are busy. A 7-day follow-up is professional; a 3-day follow-up looks anxious. Mark follow-up dates in the tracker.

→

Track your response rate. If you've sent 30+ applications with under 5% response rate, the problem isn't the companies — it's the CV or cover letter. The data tells you.

→

"Ghosted" ≠ rejected. After 14 days of no response, move the stage to "Ghosted" and send one final follow-up. If no reply: close it mentally and move on.

→

Use the Offer Comparison to negotiate. Having 2+ offers in the comparison table gives you real leverage. Even one competing offer (at any stage) strengthens your negotiating position significantly.

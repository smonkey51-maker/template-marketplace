Digital Product
Launch Checklist

Every step from idea to first sale. Use this every time you launch — no step skipped, no launch forgotten.

🎯

Phase 1: Product Foundation

Week 1–2
Validate demand before buildingSearch Gumroad, Etsy, Google for similar products. Find at least 3 selling products in your space.
Define the exact transformationComplete: "After using this product, the buyer will be able to ___ without ___." One sentence. Specific.
Choose format: PDF / template / spreadsheet / prompt packPick the simplest format that delivers the transformation. Don't over-engineer it.
Set a completion deadline (max 1 week away)Without a deadline, "almost done" is your permanent status.
Set price range before building CRITICAL$7–15 for beginner guides. $15–30 for template packs. $25–50 for comprehensive systems. Don't price after — you'll underprice from insecurity.
🔨

Phase 2: Build the Product

Week 2–3
Write the outline first (20 minutes max)Headings only. If you can't outline it in 20 minutes, the scope is too big.
Write all content (use AI to accelerate first draft)ChatGPT/Claude for draft → you edit for voice and accuracy. 70% faster.
Review: Does it deliver on the transformation you defined?Read it as if you're the buyer. Does it actually solve the problem? Cut anything that doesn't serve this.
Design in Canva (clean, minimal)Use a professional template. Change to 2–3 colors. Readable font. Export as PDF.
Have 1 person read it who matches your ideal buyerAsk them: "Was anything unclear? What would make you more confident paying for this?"
Final edit: cut all filler, tighten all instructions
Save final version as PDF (for delivery) and source file (for edits)
🛒

Phase 3: Gumroad Setup

Day before launch
Create Gumroad product and upload PDF file
Set price (do NOT use "pay what you want" for your first product) CRITICALA set price creates perceived value. PWYW attracts $0 purchases and devalues your work.
Write product description (hook → problem → what's inside → who it's for → CTA)
Create cover image (1280x720px, Canva)Include: product name, 1-line benefit, clean design. No stock photo collages.
Write 3–5 bullet points of what's included
Add "summary" (shown in search results) — 1–2 sentences
Set product URL (clean, readable: gumroad.com/l/your-product-name)
Test purchase yourself (use a promo code for 100% off) CRITICALDownload your own product. Make sure the file opens correctly and looks right.
Connect Stripe or PayPal to receive payouts
📢

Phase 4: Pre-Launch Warm-Up

3–5 days before launch
Share "behind the scenes" content about building the productNot the product itself yet — the process. This builds curiosity and investment.
Post about the PROBLEM your product solves (not the product)2–3 pieces of content that establish you understand this problem deeply.
DM 5–10 people who'd be your ideal buyer: "I'm launching something relevant to you soon"
Update your bio to mention the upcoming product
Set launch date and time (concrete: "Tuesday 10am")
🚀

Phase 5: Launch Day

Launch day
Post your launch announcement (hook + what it is + link) CRITICAL
Update your bio link to the product page
DM the warm leads you prepped: "It's live"
Share in relevant communities (subreddits, Facebook groups, Slack groups) if allowed
Post a story/short-form with the link (use "link in bio" + sticker)
Reply to every comment and DM within 2 hours
📈

Phase 6: Post-Launch (Days 2–14)

After launch
Send thank-you DM to every buyerPersonal, brief. Ask how they're finding it. This converts to a testimonial and loyalty.
Request 3 testimonials from early buyersWait 3–5 days after purchase. Ask for 2 sentences. Offer to tag them.
Add testimonials to Gumroad page as they arrive
Post 2–3x more about the topic (not just the product) — content that earns the product
Check Gumroad analytics: where are buyers coming from?Double down on the highest-converting source.
After 10 sales: consider raising the price by $3–5
After 30 days: review what worked. Start planning product 2 based on buyer feedback.

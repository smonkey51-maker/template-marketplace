1. Threads Hooks

5 prompts for opening lines that stop the scroll

Prompt 01
Hook Generator (10 variations)
Write 10 Threads hook variations for a post about [topic]. Use these 5 styles, 2 hooks each: (1) bold counterintuitive claim, (2) "I used to think X, now I know Y", (3) number-based ("After X months of Y, here's what I learned"), (4) direct address ("If you're a [target person], read this"), (5) cliffhanger. Each hook max 2 lines. No emojis unless it helps. No generic openers like "Here's the thing."
Prompt 02
Niche Hook From Experience
Write a Threads hook for a post where I share that [personal experience or result]. The hook should feel like a real person talking, not a brand. Start mid-story, not at the beginning. Make someone curious enough to read on. My niche: [your niche]. Target reader: [describe them]. Max 3 sentences.
Prompt 03
Controversial Opinion Hook
Write a Threads hook for a mildly controversial opinion in the [niche] space: [state your opinion]. The hook should state the opinion clearly without being obnoxious. It should make people who agree want to share it, and people who disagree want to respond. 1–2 sentences. No qualifiers like "I might be wrong but..."
Prompt 04
Story-Led Hook
I want to write a Threads post that starts with a mini-story before the insight. The story: [describe in 1–2 sentences what happened]. Write 5 hook variations that drop the reader into the middle of this story. Start with action or dialogue — not "So I was..." or "One day..." Max 2 sentences per hook.
Prompt 05
Problem Agitation Hook
Write 5 Threads hooks that agitate the pain of [specific problem your audience has]. Each hook should make the reader feel seen and understood — not lectured. Use "you" language. Avoid platitudes. The goal: reader thinks "this person gets it." Max 2 sentences each.

2. Full Threads Posts

5 prompts for complete scroll-stopping posts

Prompt 06
Listicle Post
Write a Threads post structured as "[number] things I learned about [topic] after [timeframe/experience]." Each point should be 1–3 lines: a bold claim, then 1–2 sentences of evidence or nuance. Hook first. End with a punchy closing line or call to action. Keep the total post under 300 words. My niche: [niche]. Target: [audience].
Prompt 07
Before/After Post
Write a Threads post showing a before/after transformation for [specific situation]. Format: Before (what I/they used to do), turning point (what changed), After (what life/results look like now), lesson (one sentence). Hook should be the "after" result — start with the transformation, then explain how. Under 250 words.
Prompt 08
Myth-Busting Post
Write a Threads post debunking [common myth in your niche]. Format: state the myth (1 line), why people believe it (2-3 lines), why it's wrong or incomplete (main body, 4–6 points or paragraphs), and what's actually true. End with the practical takeaway. Voice: confident but not condescending. Under 300 words.
Prompt 09
Value-Dense Thread
Write a long Threads post (suitable for a single long post or thread) on [topic]. Make it the most useful thing someone could read on this topic in 5 minutes. Include: at least one surprising insight, at least one actionable step, at least one specific example, and a strong close. No filler. My niche: [niche]. I want to be seen as someone who actually knows this stuff.
Prompt 10
Reply Magnet Post
Write a Threads post designed to generate replies and discussion on the topic of [topic]. End with a question that's easy to answer with 1–2 sentences — not a yes/no. The post content should be opinionated or share a personal experience to earn the right to ask the question. Under 200 words. Hook at the top.

3. Instagram Captions

5 prompts for captions that drive saves and follows

Prompt 11
Educational Caption
Write an Instagram caption for a post about [topic]. The post image/reel shows [describe visual]. The caption should: lead with a hook (1 line), deliver value in 3–5 short paragraphs, end with a CTA. The save-worthy insight: [what you're teaching]. Tone: [casual/professional/warm]. Hashtag suggestions: include 5–8 niche hashtags at the end.
Prompt 12
Product Soft-Sell Caption
Write an Instagram caption that sells [product name] without sounding like an ad. Lead with a relatable story or insight related to the problem [product] solves. Introduce the product naturally in the middle, not the opening. End with a link-in-bio CTA that feels like a recommendation, not a pitch. Under 200 words. Tone: genuine, like a trusted friend.
Prompt 13
Behind-The-Scenes Caption
Write an Instagram caption for a behind-the-scenes [photo/reel] showing [what you're showing]. Make it feel raw and real — like you're letting people in, not performing for them. Share one thing that surprised you, one thing that was harder than expected, and one thing you're proud of. CTA: follow for more of this process. Under 180 words.
Prompt 14
Carousel Caption (Save-Worthy)
Write an Instagram caption for a carousel post titled "[carousel title]." The caption should: hook with the main benefit of reading the carousel, mention that it's a [X]-slide carousel (creates expectation), and tell them to save it before they scroll. Under 100 words — the carousel IS the content, the caption just frames it.
Prompt 15
Engagement Caption
Write 5 different Instagram caption closers (final 1–2 lines) designed to maximize comments. Each should ask a question in a different style: (1) opinion question, (2) experience question, (3) this-or-that choice, (4) fill-in-the-blank, (5) "tell me I'm not the only one" validation question. For the niche: [your niche].

4. Product Descriptions

5 prompts for Gumroad, Etsy, and sales pages

Prompt 16
Gumroad Product Description
Write a Gumroad product description for [product name], a [type of product] that helps [target person] [achieve outcome]. Structure: headline (benefit-focused), 3-sentence hook (problem → agitation → product), bullet list of what's included (5–7 items), who it's for, who it's NOT for, and a CTA. Price: [price]. Tone: direct, honest, no hype. The buyer should feel like they understand exactly what they're getting.
Prompt 17
Etsy Listing Description
Write an Etsy listing description for a digital product: [product name]. It's a [type: PDF/Notion template/spreadsheet] that [what it does]. Target buyer: [describe]. Write in a warm, personal tone that feels handmade-seller authentic. Include: what they get, how it works, how to access it after purchase, and answer these common buyer questions: Is this for beginners? Can I print it? Do I need [specific software]? End with a short FAQ section.
Prompt 18
Email Announcement for New Product
Write a product launch email for [product name] to send to my email list. Subject line options: give me 5. Email body: open with a story or insight (not "I'm excited to announce"), introduce the product naturally, explain who it's for and the main benefit, list 3 things it includes, end with the purchase link and a soft deadline (if any). Under 300 words. Tone: [your brand tone].
Prompt 19
Sales Page Hero Section
Write the hero section copy for a sales page for [product name]. Include: headline (outcome-focused, max 10 words), subheadline (explains what it is and who it's for, 1–2 sentences), 3 bullet points of the biggest benefits, and a CTA button label. The visitor should understand in 10 seconds: what this is, who it's for, and why they should care. No puns or clever wordplay — clarity over cleverness.
Prompt 20
Objection-Busting FAQ
Write 6 FAQ questions and answers for the sales page of [product name], a [describe product]. The FAQs should address: (1) "Is this for beginners?", (2) the most common "but what if I..." concern, (3) the value vs. price question, (4) a technical question about access/format, (5) what they need in order to use it, (6) the refund/guarantee policy. Answers should be honest and under 60 words each.

5. Content Planning

5 prompts for building a content calendar and strategy

Prompt 21
30-Day Content Calendar
Create a 30-day content calendar for a [niche] creator on [platform: Threads/Instagram/TikTok]. My 3 content pillars are: [pillar 1], [pillar 2], [pillar 3]. I post [X times per week]. For each post slot, give me: content type (educational, story, soft sell, engagement), topic idea, and hook. Format as a table: Day | Type | Topic | Hook.
Prompt 22
Content Repurposing Plan
I have this long-form content piece: [describe or paste the article/video/podcast]. Help me repurpose it into: 3 Threads posts (use specific quotes or insights), 2 Instagram carousel ideas (titles + 5-slide structure each), 1 email newsletter section, and 3 short-form hooks for Reels or TikTok. Each format should feel native to the platform, not copy-pasted.
Prompt 23
Evergreen Content Ideas
Generate 20 evergreen content ideas for a creator in the [niche] space. "Evergreen" means: relevant any month of the year, not tied to trends or news, searchable. Format: topic title + one sentence on why this topic consistently attracts the target audience of [describe audience]. Prioritize ideas that would make someone save or bookmark the post.
Prompt 24
Content Audit Analyzer
Here are my last 10 posts and their engagement stats: [paste data]. Analyze: (1) which content types performed best, (2) what topics resonated most, (3) what posting patterns worked or didn't, (4) what I should do more of, (5) what I should stop or reduce. Give me 3 specific recommendations for next month based on this data.
Prompt 25
Newsletter Subject Lines
Write 15 email subject line options for a newsletter issue about [topic]. Mix these styles: curiosity gap, specific number/list, bold claim, personal story opener, question, "this week I learned", and plain-spoken direct. The audience: [describe]. Avoid: "exciting news," "I'm thrilled," clickbait that overpromises. Rate your top 3 picks and explain why.

6. Repurposing & Scripting

5 prompts for video scripts and content expansion

Prompt 26
60-Second Reel/TikTok Script
Write a 60-second script for a [Reel/TikTok] about [topic]. Format: hook (first 3 seconds — must stop scroll), problem setup (5–10s), main value/tip (30–40s), call-to-action (final 5s). I don't show my face — the format is text-on-screen or voiceover only. Write the script in segments with timing markers. Keep language conversational and punchy.
Prompt 27
YouTube Short Script
Write a YouTube Shorts script (under 60 seconds) about [topic]. Hook in the first line — no intro, no "hey guys." Main content: 3 fast, specific tips or insights. End: "Follow for more [topic] tips" or similar. Include a text-on-screen indicator in [brackets] for key points. The whole script should be speakable in 60 seconds at normal pace.
Prompt 28
Carousel Slide Copy
Write the slide copy for a [X]-slide Instagram or LinkedIn carousel on [topic]. Slide 1: hook (4–6 words, creates curiosity). Slides 2–[X-1]: one insight per slide, max 20 words per slide, formatted for mobile reading. Last slide: CTA (follow, save, comment, or visit link). Each slide should feel like it earns the swipe to the next.
Prompt 29
Podcast Intro Script
Write a 60-second podcast intro for an episode about [episode topic]. Include: a hook (surprising stat, question, or bold claim), a 1-sentence tease of the main insight, who the episode is for, and a transition into the episode. No "welcome back to the show" opener — start with the topic immediately. The listener should be fully hooked before the intro ends.
Prompt 30
Content Idea From Any Conversation
Turn this conversation/message/comment I received into content ideas: [paste the conversation or message]. Extract: (1) the underlying question or problem, (2) 3 Threads/social post ideas that address it, (3) 1 longer-form piece (newsletter/blog) angle, (4) a product idea if the problem is common enough. The goal: use real audience questions as content fuel.

What You Need

This program requires minimal equipment. Everything below has a bodyweight alternative — noted in the program with BW alt.

Resistance BandsLight, medium, heavy
Dumbbells2 pairs or adjustable
Pull-Up BarDoorframe style, $20–30
Yoga MatOr any non-slip floor
Sturdy ChairOr bench substitute
Water BottleNon-negotiable
No Equipment at All? Every exercise has a bodyweight-only alternative. Look for BW alt next to exercises. You can complete this entire program with zero equipment — progress just comes from reps and tempo instead of weight.

Focus: Learn movement patterns. Perfect form over speed or weight. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.

🎯 Week 1 Goal: Complete all 3 sessions. Log your starting weights. Don't go to failure — stop 2 reps before you can't continue (RPE 7).

🎯 Week 3 Goal: Hit failure (RPE 9–10) on the LAST set of your main exercises only. Log everything — this is your benchmark week.

Week 4 — Deload & Consolidation

Focus: Active recovery. 60% of Week 3 intensity. Let adaptations solidify. Prioritize mobility and technique.

🎯 Week 4 Goal: Do NOT push hard. This is intentional — reduced volume allows your muscles to grow and recover. Most beginners skip deloads and plateau. Don't.
Day 1, 2, 3
Repeat Week 1 Workouts — 60% Effort
Deload

Use the same exercises as Week 1, but:

  • Drop weight by 30–40%
  • Stop 4–5 reps before failure (RPE 6)
  • Add 30 seconds to rest periods
  • Focus entirely on perfect movement mechanics
  • Add 10 minutes of static stretching after each session

🔄 After Week 4 — What's Next?

Repeat the 4-week cycle starting at Week 1, but increase all starting weights by 5–10%. This is progressive overload — the fundamental mechanism of getting stronger.

Nutrition Basics — What to Eat Around Workouts

⏰ Pre-Workout (60–90 min before)

  • Complex carbs: oats, rice, banana
  • Moderate protein: Greek yogurt, eggs
  • Low fat (slows digestion)
  • Hydrate: 500ml water in the hour before

💪 Post-Workout (within 2 hours)

  • Fast-acting carbs: white rice, fruit
  • High-quality protein: chicken, fish, tofu
  • Target 30–40g protein in this window
  • Rehydrate: 500ml water minimum

🥩 Daily Protein Target

  • Aim for 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight
  • Split across 3–4 meals for best absorption
  • Sources: eggs, chicken, fish, legumes, dairy
  • Supplements optional — food comes first

💧 Hydration

  • Minimum 2.5 liters per day on rest days
  • 3+ liters on training days
  • Urine color: pale yellow = well hydrated
  • Avoid training fasted if you feel weak

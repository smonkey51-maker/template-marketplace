Essential Midjourney Parameters (Add to Any Prompt)

Landscape — best for product showcase images

--ar 1:1

--ar 3:4

Portrait — ideal for Etsy listings

--style raw

Less AI-stylized, more photorealistic output

--q 2

Higher quality render (costs more credits)

--no text, watermark

Removes unwanted text from image

--s 50

Lower stylization = more literal prompt following

--chaos 10

Adds variation in results (0–100)

Pro Workflow: How to Use These Mockups 1. Generate the background/setting in Midjourney using these prompts. 2. Screenshot your actual product (Notion page, spreadsheet, PDF). 3. Open Canva → upload the Midjourney background. 4. Place your product screenshot over the screen/page area. 5. Adjust size, add a drop shadow. Done. Total time: ~10 minutes per mockup.

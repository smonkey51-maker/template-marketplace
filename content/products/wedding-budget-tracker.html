💡 Budget Tips for Couples

→

Pay deposits by credit card where possible — you get consumer protection if a vendor cancels or goes under.

→

Ask for an itemized quote from every vendor. Packages that bundle everything are harder to negotiate. Individual line items give you flexibility.

→

Book vendors in this order: venue first (sets guest count), then catering (depends on venue capacity), then photographer, then everything else.

→

Track deposits separately. Deposits are rarely refundable — knowing exactly what you've committed to (not just "total cost") prevents cash flow surprises.

→

Set a Google Sheets reminder for every balance due date — 2 weeks before the due date so you have time to transfer funds.

Important Notes

→

Your rate is a floor, not a ceiling. As you gain clients, testimonials, and results — raise it. Recalculate every 6 months.

→

Project rates vs. hourly rates: Once you know your hourly rate, multiply by estimated project hours to get a project price. Add 20% buffer for surprises.

→

If the number feels too high: That's normal. Your rate feeling uncomfortable is usually evidence that you're charging correctly, not incorrectly.

→

For retainers: Calculate your monthly billable hours × recommended rate = retainer floor. Add 15% for the reliability premium.

✦ 7-DAY PROTOCOL · DAILY ACTIONS · BUILT FOR CREATORS

Social Media
Detox Protocol

A structured 7-day plan to break compulsive scrolling, reset your attention, and rebuild an intentional relationship with social media.

Day 1 — Monday
Audit & Awareness
Theme: Know exactly where your time goes before you change anything.

Morning (before opening any app)

Check your screen time report (Settings → Screen Time on iPhone / Digital Wellbeing on Android). Write down your daily average for each social app.

Set a specific intention: write one sentence starting with "This week, I want to use social media for [purpose] and not for [escape]."

During the Day

Every time you pick up your phone to scroll, pause for 3 seconds and ask: "What am I feeling right now?" Note the emotion (boredom, anxiety, loneliness, habit). Don't stop scrolling yet — just observe.

Count how many times you open Instagram/TikTok/Threads today. Just count. Don't judge.

Evening

Turn off all social media notifications for the next 7 days. Push notifications are the core trigger loop — removing them is non-negotiable.

Move social media apps to a folder on page 2 of your home screen. Not deleted — just not immediately visible.

Day 1 Reflection Prompt

"What emotion most often drives me to open social media? When do I reach for my phone instead of sitting with a feeling?"

Day 2 — Tuesday
The First Restriction
Theme: Introduce your first real limit. Expect discomfort — that's the point.

Morning

Do not open any social media app for the first 60 minutes after waking. Phone in another room if needed. Replace with: water, a 10-minute walk, or reading 5 pages of a physical book.

Set app time limits: Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X to 30 minutes each for today.

During the Day

When you hit your 30-minute limit and feel the urge to override it — don't. Let the discomfort exist for 5 minutes before deciding. Write down what you were going to check.

Replace one scrolling session with a 15-minute task you've been avoiding. Notice how you feel after vs. how you feel after scrolling.

Evening

No social media after 8 PM. The blue light and comparison loop before sleep destroys sleep quality and morning mood.

Day 2 Reflection Prompt

"What did I notice when the app timer cut me off? Was it genuine discomfort or just habit expecting to be fed?"

Day 3 — Wednesday
The Unfollow Audit
Theme: Your feed is a choice. An anxious, comparison-triggering feed is a self-inflicted wound.

Morning

Continue: no social media for 60 minutes after waking. 30-minute daily limit per app.

The Unfollow Session (20 minutes max)

Open Instagram. For every account you see: ask one question — "Does following this person make me feel inspired and good, or worse about myself?" Unfollow without guilt if the answer is the latter.

Unfollow every account that makes you feel: behind in life, not enough financially, not attractive enough, envious, or triggered. This is curating your mental diet, not cancelling people.

Mute any account you can't unfollow (close friends, colleagues) but whose content drains you.

Follow 3–5 accounts that teach you something, make you laugh genuinely, or share work in your field. Replace noise with signal.

Day 3 Reflection Prompt

"After the unfollow audit, what kind of person do I actually want to be influenced by? What values do I want reinforced daily?"

Day 4 — Thursday
Full Withdrawal Day
Theme: No social media for the full day. This is the hardest day. Do it anyway.

The Rules for Today

Zero social media. Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, Reddit — none of them. Email and messaging are allowed.

If you need to post for work/business: schedule it the night before using a scheduler. You don't need to open the app.

When the urge hits (it will, probably 20+ times): pick up a pen and write what you were going to look at. That moment of friction breaks the automatic behavior.

Replace the Time With

One activity that requires your hands: cooking, walking, drawing, organizing a space, playing an instrument.

One deep work session of at least 90 minutes with phone in another room.

One real conversation — in person or video call — with someone you care about.

Day 4 Reflection Prompt

"How many times did I feel the pull today, and what was actually underneath it each time? Boredom, loneliness, anxiety, procrastination?"

Day 5 — Friday
Intentional Re-Entry
Theme: Return to social media — but on your terms, not the algorithm's.

The New Rules (Starting Today)

Social media is allowed today — but only in designated windows: 12–12:30 PM and 6–6:30 PM. That's it. Set alarms.

Before opening any app, state your purpose aloud or in writing: "I'm going on Instagram to [specific action]." No purpose = don't open it.

No mindless feed scrolling. Only: intentional posting, responding to DMs/comments, or searching for something specific.

Notice the Difference

After your 30-minute window: write 2 sentences on how you feel vs. how you feel after an unlimited scrolling session.

Day 5 Reflection Prompt

"With a time limit and an intention, did social media feel different? More or less satisfying? More or less anxious?"

Day 6 — Saturday
Build Your Replacement Stack
Theme: The detox only sticks if you build something better to replace the habit.

Design Your Alternatives

Write a list of 10 things you enjoy that don't involve a screen. Be specific: not "exercise" but "30-minute walk listening to a podcast."

Identify your 3 highest-trigger moments: when you most automatically reach for your phone (waking up, waiting, boredom, after lunch, before bed). For each, write the alternative behavior you'll use instead.

Set up your environment: charger outside the bedroom, physical book on your nightstand, phone in your bag during meals.

Digital Hygiene Actions

Delete any apps you haven't actively decided to keep. Not muted — deleted. You can reinstall; friction is the point.

Keep notifications OFF permanently for social apps. Check on YOUR schedule, not when they summon you.

Day 6 Reflection Prompt

"What replacement activities actually worked this week? What did I discover about how I want to spend unstructured time?"

Day 7 — Sunday
Your New Rules — Permanent Setup
Theme: Write your personal social media policy for the next 30 days. Review it in a month.

Write Your Personal Policy

I will use social media at these times only: [fill in 1–2 daily windows]

My maximum daily social media time is: [X minutes]

I will not open social media in these situations: [before 9am / after 9pm / during meals / first thing when bored]

If I'm creating content for business, I schedule and post — I don't browse.

I review my screen time every Sunday. If I'm over [X minutes/day], I do a 24-hour reset.

Final Actions

Screenshot or print your policy. Put it on your phone background or inside your journal cover.

Tell one person about your new rules — accountability increases follow-through by 65%.

Day 7 Final Reflection

"What changed this week — in my focus, my mood, my time? What's one thing I want to protect now that I've felt what intentional attention feels like?"

After Day 7 — Keeping It

1

The 24-Hour Rule: Any week your screen time creeps above your personal limit, do a full 24-hour detox the following Monday. This resets the pattern before it becomes a rut.

2

Notifications Stay Off — Forever: This is the single highest-leverage change from this protocol. Don't undo it.

3

Feed = Diet: Audit your follows every 3 months. The algorithm evolves; so should your curation.

4

Scheduled Windows Are Non-Negotiable: The moment you remove the windows, the habit creeps back. Time boundaries are the infrastructure of intentional use.

5

Don't Be Precious About It: You'll slip. Everyone does. The rule isn't "never slip" — it's "notice when you slip and reset the same day, not next week."
